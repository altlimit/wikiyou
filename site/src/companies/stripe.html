Overview

Stripe, Inc. is an Irish-American financial technology company that develops payment-processing software and application programming interfaces (APIs) for businesses operating online. Its tools allow companies to accept payments, manage money, and handle related financial operations over the internet. The company is dual-headquartered in South San Francisco, California, and Dublin, Ireland, and remains privately held.

Stripe's products are widely used by internet businesses of varying sizes to process online payments, run billing systems, and manage other aspects of their financial operations. Rather than offering a single consumer-facing product, the company provides infrastructure that other businesses integrate into their own websites and applications.

History

Stripe was founded in 2010 by brothers Patrick Collison and John Collison. The company was established to simplify the process of accepting payments online, an area that had historically required businesses to navigate complex and fragmented systems. From its founding, Stripe focused on providing software that developers could integrate directly into their own products.

The company grew from these origins into a business with operations spanning both the United States and Ireland, reflecting the founders' Irish background and the company's international orientation. It maintains dual headquarters in South San Francisco, California, and Dublin, Ireland.

Products

Stripe's core offering is a set of payment-processing tools built around application programming interfaces (APIs). These APIs allow businesses to embed payment functionality into their websites and applications without building the underlying financial infrastructure themselves. The company's products are aimed primarily at internet businesses that need to handle transactions online.

  • Payment Processing — Software that enables businesses to accept payments from customers over the internet, forming the foundation of the company's product line.
  • APIs — Programmatic interfaces that developers integrate into their own systems to add payment and money-management capabilities.
  • Billing — Tools for managing recurring charges, subscriptions, and other billing arrangements for online businesses.
  • Financial Operations — Additional software that helps businesses manage money and related financial tasks online.

Because its products function as infrastructure, Stripe's software often operates behind the scenes of the websites and applications that use it. Businesses adopt these tools to manage online payments, billing, and related financial processes as part of their day-to-day operations.
