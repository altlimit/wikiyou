Overview

Anistratenco develops software for exploring health information and organizing personal context over time. Its consumer product, Health Assistant, brings questions, supported records and cited research into a conversational web workspace. HealthOS is the agentic system beneath the product.[1]

Health Assistant

The web interface includes Chat, Reflect and Research modes, voice input, supported file uploads, projects and a queue for follow-up questions. Users can bring selected information into a conversation and follow the sources behind research results. File formats, analysis capabilities and usage limits depend on the format and plan.[1]

Personal context and product scope

The product uses a health avatar: an evolving collection of the history, goals, measurements, documents and context a user chooses to share. Supported private context stays in the browser by default; selected processing or synchronization can involve servers and service providers. Health Assistant supplies consumer health information and educational support. It is not a diagnosis, prescribing, emergency-response or continuous clinical-monitoring service.[1][2]

Development direction

The broader platform direction is longitudinal health intelligence: connecting evidence with personal context so later questions can build on earlier information. Native mobile and watch applications, direct wearable connections and more proactive workflows are planned developments. Supported wearable exports can be uploaded today; that file-based workflow is distinct from the planned direct integrations.[1]
