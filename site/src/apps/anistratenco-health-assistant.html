Overview

Anistratenco Health Assistant is a consumer health-information web application developed by Anistratenco. It combines questions, user-selected records and source-linked research in a conversational workspace. HealthOS provides the agentic system beneath the application.[1]

Interface and workflows

Users can ask written questions, use voice input, upload supported files and organize conversations in projects. Chat, Reflect and Research modes offer different levels of inquiry. A question queue lets a user collect follow-up questions while researching. Research results can link to the sources used in an answer.[1]

Records and continuity

Supported uploads include common documents, spreadsheets, structured data and images. The application can also work with supported exports from health or fitness applications. Its health avatar brings together the history, goals, measurements, documents and context selected by the user. Supported private context stays in the browser by default, while features involving remote processing or synchronization send information needed for that purpose.[1][2]

Scope and planned development

Health Assistant provides educational health information. It does not diagnose, prescribe, respond to emergencies or provide continuous clinical monitoring. Native mobile and watch apps, direct wearable integrations and more proactive workflows are planned features. Importing an available wearable export does not imply that a direct connection or ongoing monitoring service has launched.[1]
