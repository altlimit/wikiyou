What this converter does

This tool converts pounds to grams using the exact relationship between the international avoirdupois pound and the gram. It is useful for cooking, shipping weights, nutrition labels, classroom unit conversion, and quick everyday calculations where a pound value needs to be expressed in grams.

The formula

The exact formula is:

grams = pounds × 453.59237

For everyday use, many people round one pound to 454 grams. The exact value is better when you are scaling a recipe, checking nutrition values, or comparing measurements that should be repeatable.
